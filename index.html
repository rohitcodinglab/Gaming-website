About

I started making games by modding levels into a racing game at fourteen, and never really stopped taking things apart to see how they worked. These days that curiosity looks like combat systems, save architectures, and the occasional three-day rabbit hole into cloth physics.

I care most about the moments a player doesn't consciously notice — the weight of a jump, the read on an enemy's wind-up, the silence right before a boss fight starts. Most of my work happens in Unity and Unreal, with some tool-building in Python along the way.

Based in Vancouver, working with studios remotely and shipping solo projects on the side.
